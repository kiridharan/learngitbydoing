# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all people, regardless of gender identity and expression, sexual orientation, disabilities, neurodiversity, physical appearance, body size, ethnicity, nationality, race, age, religion, or other identity category.

We invite all those who participate in Git-D3-Viz to help us create a safe and positive community experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- **Be Respectful**: Treat everyone with respect and dignity. Be kind and courteous in all interactions.
- **Be Inclusive**: Welcome and support people of all backgrounds and identities.
- **Be Collaborative**: Listen actively, consider different perspectives, and work constructively toward common goals.
- **Give Credit**: Acknowledge and appreciate contributions from others.
- **Be Professional**: Keep discussions focused, constructive, and professional.
- **Assume Good Intent**: Interpret actions charitably and ask clarifying questions before jumping to conclusions.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- **Harassment**: Sustained or targeted harassment, intimidation, or bullying of any kind.
- **Discrimination**: Discriminatory jokes, slurs, or comments based on protected characteristics.
- **Violence and Threats**: Violence, threats of violence, or violent language directed at others.
- **Unwelcome Sexual Attention**: Unwelcome or inappropriate sexual comments, contact, or behavior.
- **Disruption**: Deliberate disruption of discussions, events, or community spaces.
- **Doxxing**: Sharing private information (name, address, phone, email, etc.) without consent.
- **Spam**: Posting repetitive, off-topic, or promotional content.
- **Abuse**: Personal attacks, insults, or demeaning language.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:
- All community spaces (GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, wiki)
- Official community events and meetings
- Unofficial events representing the community
- Any communication related to this project

## Enforcement

### Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it by:

1. **Email**: Send a detailed report to the project maintainers
2. **Private Message**: Contact a maintainer directly on GitHub
3. **GitHub Issues**: For less severe concerns, you may open an issue (though private reporting is preferred for sensitive matters)

**Please include:**
- Description of the behavior
- Context and date
- Who was involved
- How the violation affected you
- Any relevant links or evidence

All reports will be kept confidential.

### Response

Upon receiving a report, the maintainers will:

1. Acknowledge receipt within 48 hours
2. Investigate the report fairly and thoroughly
3. Determine appropriate action
4. Communicate the outcome to affected parties

### Consequences

Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in:

- **Warning**: A private or public warning about the behavior
- **Temporary Ban**: Temporary suspension from the community (1-30 days)
- **Permanent Ban**: Permanent removal from the community
- **Legal Action**: If applicable, escalation to appropriate authorities

The severity of consequences will match the severity of the violation and take into account:
- The nature and impact of the violation
- Whether it was intentional or accidental
- The person's history of violations
- Willingness to make amends

## Appeals

If you believe you have been unfairly penalized, you may appeal to the project maintainers within 30 days with additional context or information.
